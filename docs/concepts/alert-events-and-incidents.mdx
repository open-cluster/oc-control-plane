---
title: "Incidents and Alert Events"
description: "Learn how normalized alert occurrences form provisional Incidents."
---

An Alert Event is one normalized source occurrence. Its source timestamp remains distinct from receipt time, and redelivery is idempotent.

An Incident groups Alert Events using the alert source's grouping identity. Grouping is provisional and is never a causal claim. The Incident remains firing while any grouped Alert Event is firing and resolves when none are firing.

Use an Incident to review its timeline, linked Conversations, and Investigations before drawing a conclusion.

For example, three firing Alert Events with the same Alertmanager grouping key can update
one Incident. Resolving two does not resolve the Incident while the third is firing. This
grouping says the alerts arrived as one operational episode; it does not prove they share
a root cause.

Provider grouping quality limits Incident quality. Merge or review provisional grouping
before treating it as causal evidence. Continue with
[Run your first investigation](/getting-started/run-your-first-investigation).
