---
title: "Postmortems"
description: "Generate and review conservative draft Postmortems for resolved Incidents."
---

A Postmortem belongs to one resolved Incident. An operator triggers generation; OpenCluster does not create one automatically.

The draft distinguishes system evidence from Conversation testimony and remains `draft` until a person reviews it. Missing impact, resolution, owner, or deadline facts appear exactly as `Needs human input.` rather than being invented.

Postmortems remain under Incidents in v0.1. Review every section, correct explicit human facts, and then mark the document reviewed.

Generation assembles the executive summary, impact, detection, cited timeline, root
causes, contributing factors, resolution, what went well or poorly, detection gaps,
Action Items, and open questions. A correction creates a new revision; marking it
`reviewed` records the reviewer without turning testimony into system evidence.

For example, Tool Runs may prove when an Alert Event fired and when a deployment changed,
while an incident commander supplies the customer impact and recovery decision. The
draft cites the former and labels absent human facts with `Needs human input.`.

A Postmortem is not automatically published, is not an execution plan, and cannot recover
facts that were never observed or supplied. Resolve the Incident first, generate its
nested draft, review every uncited statement, and retain human ownership of Action Items.
