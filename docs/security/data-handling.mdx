---
title: "Data handling"
description: "See which operational records OpenCluster stores and what it deliberately excludes."
---

OpenCluster stores normalized Alert Events, Incidents, Messages, Investigation events, Tool Runs, cited Findings, audit records, and draft Postmortems in PostgreSQL.

Tool results are bounded and summarized for operator review. Conversations retain bounded continuity through Messages and prior cited Findings. OpenCluster does not persist or expose model chain-of-thought.

An Investigation can perform live authorized reads, including Kubernetes container logs
through a connected Relay and Slack channel or thread replies through a configured App.
The bounded question, selected source context, Tool results, and prior cited Findings may
cross the model provider boundary. Credentials and raw chain-of-thought do not.

Retention and backup policy are deployment responsibilities in OSS. Protect backups as production data and verify deletion or retention changes against legal and operational requirements.
