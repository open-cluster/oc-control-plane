---
title: "Ask follow-up questions"
description: "Keep narrowing a problem in one conversation instead of starting over each time."
---

A conversation lets you keep asking. Each question you send opens an investigation, and
every investigation after the first already knows what the ones before it read, what you
told it to focus on, and what has already been ruled out.

Use a conversation when one question is not the whole question: narrowing a cause,
checking what contradicts a theory, or asking something operational outside an incident.

## Prerequisites

- The deployment has conversations enabled.
- The deployment has a configured and consented model provider.
- You have the **Editor** or **Admin** role.

## Start a conversation

Open a conversation with a subject, and optionally the incident it is about. A conversation
started from an incident already knows the alert, so you do not have to describe it.

Send your first question and OpenCluster starts investigating. Send another when the answer
arrives, and it continues from where the last one stopped.

Several people can take part in one conversation, and every message records who sent it.
Two people looking at the same incident can also hold separate conversations, so one line of
questioning does not derail another.

## What a follow-up already knows

| Carried forward | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| What earlier questions established | The answer is not re-read and not paid for twice. |
| The reads behind each of those facts | You can still check any claim, however old. |
| Instructions you gave | "Ignore the database, look at deployments" keeps holding. |
| Hypotheses already ruled out | A dead end stays closed unless new evidence reopens it. |
| Questions left unanswered | What was not settled is not quietly forgotten. |
| The services and resources already read | A follow-up does not rediscover your estate. |

What is **not** carried forward is another conversation's messages. Two conversations about
one incident share the incident, never each other.

## Ask something that is not an incident

A conversation is also how you ask an operational question in peacetime — "which version is
currently deployed?" — and get a direct answer rather than a causal report. The answer still
cites the reads that support it.

## Send a message while OpenCluster is working

You do not have to wait for a green light. A message sent mid-investigation is accepted and
taken up as soon as the current one finishes. Send three and they are answered together, as
one question in three parts, rather than as three competing investigations.

The response tells you which happened: either the investigation it started, or that it was
queued.

## Watch an investigation as it runs

Each investigation publishes an ordered stream of what it is doing: which read is running
against which integration, what each read returned in one line, when one failed, and the
answer as it is produced.

You can close the page and come back. The stream resumes exactly where you stopped — nothing
missed and nothing repeated.

The stream never contains the model's private reasoning. Every line is written by
OpenCluster from what it did, not by the model describing itself.

## Long conversations

A conversation that runs for hours keeps working. When the record of it grows past what the
model can hold, OpenCluster consolidates the older part into a structured summary: the goal,
your instructions in your own words, established facts with their citations, what was ruled
out, and what is still open. The full transcript is never edited or deleted — only what the
model carries changes.

If a single question is too large to answer inside that budget, the investigation concludes
with what it established and says it stopped for context, exactly as it does for any other
limit. A partial answer is never presented as a finished one.

## When something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| The message was queued | An investigation is still running. Yours is next. |
| Too many investigations waiting | Your organization is at its limit. Wait for one to finish. |
| An investigation stopped for `context` | The question filled the working context. Ask a narrower one. |
| An investigation failed with "the worker running this investigation stopped" | OpenCluster restarted mid-run. Ask again; nothing was lost from the record. |

See [Understand findings and citations](/investigations/findings-and-citations) for what a
finding's type and confidence mean.
