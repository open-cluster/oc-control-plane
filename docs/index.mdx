---
title: "OpenCluster"
description: "Investigate incidents across the systems you already use, with cited findings and bounded access."
---

OpenCluster is an open-source AI SRE for investigating production incidents. It follows
bounded read-only evidence across your existing alerting, infrastructure, collaboration,
and source-control systems, keeps competing hypotheses visible, and returns a structured
conclusion you can audit.

Every material claim cites a numbered Tool Run. Action proposals state risk,
reversibility, approval needs, and verification; OpenCluster never executes a production
change. After resolution, you can generate and review a conservative draft Postmortem.
