---
title: "OpenCluster"
description: "Connect your alerting, cluster, chat and source control. When an alert fires, OpenCluster investigates — and shows its work."
---

OpenCluster turns a firing alert into an investigated incident. It receives alerts from
your existing Alertmanager, groups them into incidents, and — when you ask — runs an
autonomous investigation across your connected systems: what is happening, what probably
caused it, what changed, what was ruled out, and what to do next. Every claim cites the
exact reads that support it, so a conclusion is something you can audit, not something
you have to trust.

## What you get

- **Incidents, not alert noise.** Alerts group into incident episodes using your
  Alertmanager's own grouping, and an episode resolves itself when nothing in it still
  fires.
- **Investigations with causal structure.** A conclusion distinguishes probable cause,
  triggering change, contributing factor, symptom, ruled-out explanation and unresolved
  lead — each with a stated confidence and the reads that back it.
- **Read-only, bounded access.** OpenCluster never posts to your chat, never writes to
  your repositories, and never touches your cluster directly. Every read is bounded,
  recorded, and clamped to the incident's time window.
- **Honesty over theater.** If the evidence establishes nothing, the conclusion says so.
  If a budget ended the investigation early, the outcome is labeled as stopped, never
  dressed up as a completed diagnosis.
