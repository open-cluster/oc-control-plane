Day-two OpenCluster: what runs on its own, what an operator drives, and what to watch.

## What runs on its own

| Worker | Cadence | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audit pruner | hourly | Applies each organization's declared audit retention |
| Change-ledger pruner | hourly | Ages change entries past `OC_CHANGE_LEDGER_RETENTION_DAYS` |
| Relay inventory sync | `OC_INVENTORY_INTERVAL` (each Relay floors it) | Feeds the change ledger for Kubernetes integrations |
| Investigation runner | per opened investigation | Reads, records and concludes in the background; at most four at once per process |

## Incidents

Alerts group into incident episodes on the source's own grouping identity; an episode
resolves when nothing in it still fires, and a wrong grouping is corrected by a merge
that rewrites nothing. Splits do not exist because the grouping errs toward splitting.

## Investigations, operationally

The runner offers every enabled source whose verified grants support at least one
capability, orients the investigator with what the platform already holds, records every
read as it finishes, suppresses identical repeats, and forces a conclusion when reads
stagnate or a ceiling is reached. Spend is summed over every model call, including
refused ones. The outcome semantics — concluded, stopped-by-limit, failed — are on
[how investigations work](/investigations/how-investigations-work).

Every model call emits one trace span and one structured log line: provider, the model
that answered, request id, stop reason, latency, token decomposition, cost, and a
derived revision hash over everything the model sees — so any change to prompt or
capability definitions is attributable without a version anybody bumps.

## Health signals worth alerting on

- `readyz` unready: this instance's required database placement is unreachable.
- Integration status `failed` or `degraded` after verification — a revoked token, a
  suspended installation, a disconnected Relay, missing scopes. The note says which.
- Relay fleet summary `outdated` or `disconnected` counts.
- Investigations ending `failed` with reasons naming the model provider: check the
  deployment's model configuration and the vendor's status.

## Metrics

Prometheus series are served on `/metrics`, low-cardinality by design (never labeled by
organization). The families worth dashboards:

- `oc.intake.deliveries` by disposition (accepted, duplicate, unauthenticated,
  oversized, malformed, rate_limited, unavailable), `oc.intake.signals`, and episode
  grouping counters.
- `oc.reasoning.tokens`, `oc.reasoning.spend` and `oc.reasoning.call_duration`, labeled
  by provider and model (the exporter adds its own suffixes).

## Retention

Audit retention is each organization's own declaration, enforced hourly. The change ledger's
retention is the deployment's setting. Signals, incidents and investigation provenance
are kept: they are the operational record.
