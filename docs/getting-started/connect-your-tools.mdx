---
title: "Connect your tools"
description: "Add read-only integrations and verify what evidence OpenCluster can retrieve."
---

An Integration is one configured installation in an Organization. Each Integration exposes bounded read-only Tools; OpenCluster never receives permission to change customer systems.

1. Open **Integrations** and choose a shipped Integration Type.
2. Enter its non-secret configuration and required credential.
3. Select **Verify** and review the reported capabilities and limitations.
4. For Kubernetes, deploy the Relay inside the customer boundary and verify its outbound session.

Credentials are write-only after submission. Webhook secrets are shown once. See the [Integration overview](/integrations/overview) for provider-specific guides.

Verification reports `configured`, `active`, `degraded`, or `failed` with an explanation
based on observable provider behavior. A webhook Integration remains `configured` until
the first delivery arrives. A read Integration can be `degraded` when only some declared
capabilities are available.

For example, connect Generic Webhook, send one firing Alert Event, and verify that its
status becomes `active`. Disable an Integration to remove it from new Investigations while
retaining history. Deletion is refused when retained records depend on it.

Provider permissions and data availability limit every Tool; verification does not prove
that a future Investigation will find a cause. Continue with
[Run your first investigation](/getting-started/run-your-first-investigation).
