title: "Run your first investigation"
description: "Ask a question, review cited findings, and continue the Conversation."
---

Use an operational question or an existing Incident as your starting point. Connect a [relevant source](/integrations/overview) and configure a model provider. Editors and Admins can ask through the API and open investigations. Slack questions use the installed app.

## Ask an operational question

Start a Conversation with a specific question, such as “Is the checkout Deployment healthy in the payments namespace?” or “What errors appeared in checkout logs in the last 15 minutes?” Name the source or service when several could apply.

The shipping deployment uses the [Conversation API example](/api-reference/workflows#ask-a-question). An installed [Slack app](/integrations/collaboration/slack) can also answer direct mentions in an authorized channel thread.

## Investigate an Incident

An accepted firing alert can open an Incident and automatically queue an Investigation when a model provider is available. Follow that Investigation rather than opening a duplicate. To investigate an existing Incident again, use the [Incident investigation procedure](/api-reference/workflows#investigate-an-incident).

## Review the answer

Read the summary, cited Findings, and limitations. Citations identify the reads supporting each Finding. Review their timestamps and any incomplete results. Editors and Admins can [inspect a cited observation](/concepts/investigations-and-conversations#inspect-a-cited-observation).

A completed Investigation may answer a factual question, support an explanation, or remain inconclusive. Missing access or telemetry can prevent an answer. Action Proposals are recommendations for you to review and verify. OpenCluster does not execute production changes.

## Continue the Conversation

Ask a focused follow-up, such as “Did the deployment happen before the errors started?” Follow-ups open new investigations in the same conversation. Messages sent during active work wait and may be handled together. Earlier results stay unchanged, and prior cited Findings provide context. See [Conversations and follow-ups](/concepts/investigations-and-conversations#conversations-and-follow-ups).
